# FitGenie.AI – AI-Powered Workout and Diet Planning Platform

FitGenie.AI is an AI-powered fitness and wellness platform designed to make personal fitness management smarter, simpler, and more interactive. It combines workout planning, personalised nutrition tracking, biometric calculations, computer vision, and AI-based assistance into one integrated application.

The platform helps users understand their fitness requirements through biometric measurements such as age, height, weight, BMI, and estimated calorie requirements. It provides personalised workout and diet planning, allowing users to organise their fitness routines according to their individual goals.

One of its key features is the **Computer Vision Lab**, which uses a MoveNet-labelled pose-tracking interface to analyse body movements through a live camera feed. It displays body landmarks, joint angles, repetition counts, and potential exercise-form errors to support better movement awareness.

### Key Features
- **Biometrics Dashboard:** BMI, BMR/REE, TDEE, calorie targets, and macronutrient estimates.
- **AI Workout Planner:** Personalised workout routines based on fitness goals.
- **AI Diet Planner:** Nutrition and meal planning assistance.
- **Live AI Coach:** Interactive workout sessions and exercise tracking.
- **Computer Vision Lab:** Real-time pose tracking, joint-angle analysis, repetition counting, and form feedback.
- **Food Scanner:** Meal image-based food entry workflow.
- **Nutrition & Activity Diary:** Tracks meals, water intake, sleep, weight, and workouts.
- **Progress Tracking:** Workout history, activity calendar, and progress forecasts.
- **GenieCoach Chatbot:** Conversational fitness assistance and contextual guidance.
- **Security:** Firewall use and account/session controls.

### Project Objective
The main objective of FitGenie.AI is to bring fitness planning, nutrition management, and exercise feedback together in one platform while demonstrating the practical application of Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, and Computer Vision.
